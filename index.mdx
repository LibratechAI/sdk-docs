No CSS conflicts, no external requests at load, no auth configuration on the host side. Current release: **v{sdkVersion}** ({sdkReleaseDate}).

## Who this site is for

- **Engineers** integrating the SDK. Every guide leads with the snippet, copied verbatim from the SDK's README, then explains the behaviour around it.
- **Product managers and marketing.** The [Capabilities](/capabilities/overview) section describes what end users see, with screenshots from the playground, and [Products and sources](/capabilities/products-and-sources) lists which Wolters Kluwer products the SDK serves.

<Note>
This site is for Wolters Kluwer product teams. The SDK is published to WK Artifactory and the WK CDN, not to the public npm registry.
</Note>

## A note on names

**`@libra/sdk`** is the package you install. **The embedded widget** is what your users see. Wolters Kluwer product and design material calls that surface the **Libra Add-in** — the same thing.

These docs avoid "add-in", because in Libra's own codebase that word means the **Word and Outlook add-ins**, a genuinely separate surface whose conversations are kept in their own list and never appear in the widget's history.

<Tip>
**Before your first integration call:** every domain you embed on has to be registered with Libra in advance, including localhost and staging. It is the single most common reason a first integration appears to hang on the sign-in screen. [Authentication](/guides/authentication#register-your-domain-before-you-go-live)
</Tip>
