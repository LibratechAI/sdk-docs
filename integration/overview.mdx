The Libra SDK lets a host product embed Libra's chat experience in an isolated iframe. The host owns the surrounding product UI and chooses when and where the widget appears; the SDK owns the chat interface and its session.

This guide is for engineers and QA. To see what users experience first, read [Capabilities](/capabilities/overview).

## What the host provides

- A valid `productId` identifying your product: a legal-research product or a legal software product. See [By product type](#by-product-type).
- A host-owned open control and a visible target container.
- The surrounding panel or modal when the product uses one.
- The close behavior. Providing `onClose` enables the SDK header; the callback should hide the host wrapper and call `Libra.detach()`.

## By product type

Most of the integration is the same for every product. These parts differ between legal-research products and legal software products:

| Topic | Research product | Legal software product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `productId` | A research product ID, such as `wko_de` | A legal software product ID: `kleos`, `legisway`, `notaionext`, or `suitenext` |
| Document `source` and `id` | A research source, such as `wko_de`, and your document ID | The legal software product, and its own document ID |
| Can Libra use the document? | Only if it's already in your product's **FAB index**. Libra checks, but doesn't index. | Libra links it into the user's active project and indexes it, within 90 seconds. |
| When a suggestion is checked or linked | Checked in the background while the chip is visible | Linked only when the user selects the chip |

Details: [Product ID](/integration/lifecycle#product-id), [Attachments and availability](/integration/attachments), [Suggest an attachment](/integration/suggestions), and [Add attachments](/integration/add-attachments). The code examples on those pages have a tab for each product type.

For every product type, users can attach their own files, and the [Document Viewer](/capabilities/document-viewer) opens uploaded and generated documents beside the SDK window, including in chats reopened from history. A product without its own document source, such as `ra_micro`, can pass files with `HostFile`.
